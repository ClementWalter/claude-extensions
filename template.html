Claude Extensions

Connect Claude to your messages, email, documents and subscriptions. Each extension runs on your own Mac, with your own accounts: nothing goes through a server of ours.

{{nav}}

What these are

Extensions are add-ons for Claude Desktop (and Cowork, inside it). Once one is installed, you can ask Claude things like "what homework is due tomorrow?", "find the email from the plumber" or "read me today's paper", and Claude uses the extension to look it up.

They run on your Mac only. They read your data straight from the service you already use (WhatsApp, Gmail, Notion, your newspaper...) and keep sign-ins and copies on your Mac. Whatever Claude reads through them becomes part of your Claude conversation, like anything you paste into it.

macOS only. Each extension asks before it sends, changes or deletes anything.

How to install

  1. Install Claude Desktop for Mac from claude.ai/download and sign in.
  2. Click Download on the extension you want below. A file ending in .mcpb lands in your Downloads folder.
  3. Double-click that file. Claude Desktop opens it.
  4. Click Install. Fill in the settings if the extension has any, then follow its setup steps. The first start takes a minute while it gets ready.

To remove one: Claude Desktop, Settings, Extensions, then the extension, Uninstall.
